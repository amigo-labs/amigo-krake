# Krake Design System

## Design Context

### Users
Power users and tech enthusiasts managing downloads (HTTP, Usenet) in a centralized platform. They need instant orientation and rely on status indicators (colour with a word or an icon, badges, progress). The app is used on both desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
**Technical, Precise, Powerful**

- Voice: Direct, competent, no unnecessary words
- Tone: Calm and professional; the tool steps back behind the downloads
- Emotional goal: Confident control — everything that matters is visible, nothing shouts

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: The shared amigo app theme — the same surfaces, text, status colours, type, radii and shadows as every amigo app (design decision 16 in `AGENTS.md`)
- **Source**: amigo-labs/website `theme/` (meaning of every token: its `theme/README.md`). The generated copy lives in `packages/ui/src/theme/` and is never edited by hand; the Site data workflow fails when it differs from what the generator writes for `docs/site/project.yml`
- **Accent**: One, the brand lime `#9be22d` (`docs/site/project.yml`); there are no user palettes. The theme splits it into roles: `accent` (decorative fills), `accent-ink` (text and icons), `accent-solid` with `on-accent` (the primary button, checked controls), `accent-subtle` (a selected row, chip or nav item)
- **Palettes**: Dark is the default. The user picks Dark, Light or System in Settings ▸ Appearance; the sun / moon button in the header switches between dark and light
- **No neon**: No glow shadows, no scanlines, no flicker, no intensity setting. `scripts/ui-lint.mjs` and `packages/ui/scripts/check-theme.mjs` reject them
- **Anti-References**: Generic Bootstrap dashboards, bloated enterprise UIs, playful consumer apps

### Design Principles

1. **One Theme, One Accent**: Colours, radii, shadows and font stacks come from the shared tokens. Krake adds no colour of its own; a value that is missing is added to the theme in amigo-labs/website.
2. **Instant Orientation**: Status, speed, and progress visible at a glance. No hidden information behind clicks.
3. **Borders First**: Depth is a hairline `border` between surfaces; the neutral shadows `elev-1` to `elev-3` only for what floats (menus, dialogs, a dragged row). State is shown by the accent — an edge bar, a subtle fill, an ink colour — about a tenth of a screen.
4. **Responsive Without Compromise**: Mobile is not an afterthought — every page works at 320px just as well as at 2560px.
5. **Respect Accessibility**: `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all animations. All three text colours hold 4.5:1 on every surface, control outlines and status fills 3:1, in both palettes (`pnpm --filter @amigo-labs/krake-ui check`). A status always has a word or an icon next to its colour.

### Design Tokens

- **Files**: `packages/ui/src/theme/tokens.css` (generated, every token as `--am-*`) and `packages/ui/src/tokens.css` (Krake's property names as aliases of those tokens, plus z-index, font-size steps and motion curves)
- **Surfaces**: `--bg-deep` (the window), `--bg-surface` (panels), `--bg-surface-2` (fields and nested blocks), `--hover-bg`
- **Text**: `--text-primary`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-muted`
- **Borders**: `--border-color` (hairline), `--border-strong` (outline of a field or a toggle rail)
- **Status**: `--success` / `--warning` / `--danger` for fills and dots (`--status-online` / `--status-degraded` / `--status-error`), the `-ink` forms for text
- **Typography**: Inter (self-hosted, latin subset) and the ui-monospace stack from the theme; Krake's size steps 0.75rem - 2.5rem
- **Radii**: `--radius-xs` to `--radius-lg`, `--radius-full`; controls `sm` / `md`, panels and dialogs `lg`
- **Breakpoint**: 768px (single primary breakpoint)
- **Spacing**: the 4px grid, `--space-1` to `--space-16`; page padding 2rem / 1rem (desktop / mobile)
- **Cards**: a `--border-color` hairline; an interactive card lifts to `--elev-1` on hover
- **Theme choice**: `localStorage` `theme` = `dark` | `light` | `system` (`web-ui/src/lib/theme.ts`). The root carries `data-theme="dark"` or `"light"`; System removes the attribute and the stylesheet follows the system
